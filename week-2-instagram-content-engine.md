## PROMPT 1: Define Content Pillars (Run Once)

```
I own a pro-am dance studio (professional-amateur partnership dancing).
We recently opened a second location. Our target audience is adults,
primarily 30-60 years old, who are curious about dance but may feel
intimidated to start.

Create 5 Instagram content pillars for our studio. For each pillar:

1. Pillar name and description (one sentence)
2. Why this pillar attracts new students
3. 4 specific recurring post formats I can repeat every month
4. Example caption for one of the formats
5. Best posting format: carousel, single image, Reel, or Story

The pillars should cover this mix:
- Building trust with beginners (addressing fears)
- Showcasing the social/community side
- Student transformations and success stories
- Behind-the-scenes / day-in-the-life of the studio
- Educational content that positions us as experts

Important context:
- Pro-am means students dance with professional instructors
- It's NOT competitive ballroom only — it's also social, fun, and accessible
- Many students come for connection, confidence, and a new hobby
- Our differentiator: [ADD YOUR UNIQUE SELLING POINT - e.g., welcoming atmosphere,
  specific dance styles, event opportunities, etc.]

Language for captions: [German / English / your preference]
```

## PROMPT 2: Monthly Content Calendar

Run this on the 1st of each month. Paste your content pillars from Prompt 1.

```
Create a full Instagram content calendar for [MONTH, YEAR] for my pro-am
dance studio.

My content pillars are:
[PASTE YOUR 5 PILLARS FROM PROMPT 1]

I need:
- 4 posts per week (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat) = 16-17 posts for the month
- 4 Reels ideas for the month (one per week, can overlap with posts)
- 2 Story series ideas (multi-slide stories for engagement)

For EACH post, give me:
1. Date and day
2. Content pillar it belongs to
3. Post type: single image / carousel / Reel
4. Caption (ready to post, in [LANGUAGE])
5. Visual direction: exactly what the image or video should show
   (so my Instagram person can create or source it)
6. 3 hashtag groups (10 hashtags each): one local, one dance-specific,
   one broader lifestyle
7. CTA type: engage (question/poll), save (tip/value), share (relatable),
   or convert (book a class)

Distribution rules:
- No more than 2 posts from the same pillar in a row
- At least 2 posts per month with a direct but soft CTA to try a class
- At least 3 posts featuring real students or testimonials (I'll add names/details)
- At least 2 Reels showing actual dancing (short, engaging clips)
- Include 1 "myth-busting" post (e.g., "You don't need a partner to start")

Upcoming events or seasonal hooks for [MONTH]:
- [ADD ANY UPCOMING EVENTS: showcases, open houses, holidays, etc.]

Tone: warm, encouraging, confident but never arrogant. We're the studio
where everyone belongs.
```

## PROMPT 3: Reel Script Generator

For when you want to create short-form video content (Reels perform best for reach).

```
Write a script for a 30-second Instagram Reel for my pro-am dance studio.

Reel concept: [CHOOSE ONE]
a) "What people think pro-am dance is vs. what it actually is"
b) "Your first lesson at [STUDIO NAME]: what to expect"
c) "Student transformation: [STUDENT NAME]'s journey from nervous beginner to [result]"
d) "3 reasons adults are starting dance in 2026"
e) "[DANCE STYLE] in 30 seconds - could you do this?"
f) Custom: [YOUR IDEA]

Give me:
1. Hook (first 2 seconds - what text appears on screen to stop scrolling)
2. Scene-by-scene breakdown with timing
3. On-screen text for each scene
4. Background music mood suggestion
5. Caption for the post
6. CTA at the end

Keep it authentic - not overly produced. Phone-quality video with
good energy performs better than polished corporate content.
```

## Quick-Reference: What Makes Dance Studio Content Work

Use this as a mental checklist when reviewing content:

**Hooks that stop scrolling for dance content:**
- Transformation reveals (before/after clips)
- "I was terrified to..." (vulnerability)
- Counter-intuitive statements ("You don't need rhythm to dance")
- Numbers ("After 6 months of lessons, here's what changed")
- Direct questions ("When's the last time you tried something new?")

**What your audience wants to see:**
- Real people (not just pros), especially people who look like them
- The social side (laughing, chatting, community)
- Progress, not perfection
- What a first lesson actually looks like (remove the mystery)
- Behind-the-scenes of events and showcases

**What doesn't work:**
- Stock photos
- Overly technical dance terminology
- Content that only appeals to people who already dance
- Hard sell CTAs on every post
- Inconsistent posting (the algorithm punishes gaps)

**Posting cadence:**
- Minimum: 4x/week (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat)
- Reels: 1-2x/week (highest reach potential)
- Stories: daily when possible (keeps you top of feed)
- Best times for local business: test, but generally 7-9 AM and 6-8 PM
