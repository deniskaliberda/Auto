## PROMPT 1: Identify Target Business Categories

Copy this into Claude. Run once, then revisit quarterly.

```
I run an SEO optimization company targeting small businesses in Germany.
My focus area is [YOUR CITY/REGION].

Help me identify 10 specific types of local businesses that are ideal SEO clients.
For each type, I need:

1. The business category (be specific - not just "restaurants" but "independent restaurants
   without chain affiliation")
2. Why they need local SEO (what their customers search for)
3. Why they typically have bad SEO (what they neglect)
4. Revenue estimate - can they realistically afford €[YOUR MONTHLY PRICE]/month?
5. Exactly what Google searches I should use to find them on page 2-3

Prioritize businesses where:
- Customers search before they buy (not impulse purchases)
- The business owner feels the pain of not being found online
- There's enough margin in their business to justify SEO investment
- Competition exists but isn't dominated by big chains

Format as a numbered list. Be specific to the German market.
```

## PROMPT 2: Prospect Research from Google Results

After finding businesses on Google page 2-3, use this for each one:

```
I found this business on page 2 of Google results, which means they have
potential but poor SEO. Help me prepare for outreach.

Business name: [NAME]
Website: [URL]
Business type: [e.g., Zahnarzt, Rechtsanwalt, Restaurant]
City: [CITY]
What I searched to find them: [YOUR SEARCH QUERY]

Based on a quick look at their website, tell me:
1. What are the likely SEO problems just from the URL and business type?
2. What keywords should they be ranking for but probably aren't?
3. What's their likely biggest pain point from not ranking well?
4. What's one specific, free insight I can give them in my outreach
   that they can verify themselves?

Keep it concise - I need this to personalize a cold email.
```

## PROMPT 3: Cold Outreach Email (German)

This is your main outreach template. Personalize for each prospect.

```
Schreibe eine Kaltakquise-E-Mail auf Deutsch für meine SEO-Firma.

Angaben zum Interessenten:
- Firmenname: [NAME]
- Branche: [BRANCHE]
- Stadt: [STADT]
- Website: [URL]
- Top 3 SEO-Probleme, die ich gefunden habe:
  1. [PROBLEM 1 - aus SE Ranking Audit]
  2. [PROBLEM 2]
  3. [PROBLEM 3]

Angaben zu meiner Firma:
- Ich biete SEO-Optimierung für kleine Unternehmen in Deutschland an
- Mein Name: [DEIN NAME]
- Meine Firma: [FIRMENNAME]

Regeln für die E-Mail:
- Maximal 120 Wörter
- Zeige, dass ich mir die Website tatsächlich angesehen habe (kein generisches Template)
- Gib EINEN konkreten, kostenlosen Tipp, den sie sofort umsetzen können
- Kein aggressiver Verkaufston - professionell und menschlich
- CTA: Kostenloses 15-Minuten-Gespräch anbieten, um die Ergebnisse durchzugehen
- Betreffzeile: kurz, neugierig machend, kein Spam-Gefühl
- Keine Floskeln wie "Ich hoffe, diese E-Mail erreicht Sie wohlauf"
- Direkt zum Punkt kommen

Gib mir:
1. Betreffzeile
2. E-Mail-Text
3. Signatur-Vorschlag
```

## PROMPT 4: Follow-Up Sequence

Run this once to create your 3-email follow-up sequence, then reuse the framework.

```
Ich habe eine Kaltakquise-E-Mail an einen potenziellen SEO-Kunden geschickt
und keine Antwort erhalten.

Ursprüngliche E-Mail (zusammengefasst):
- Ich habe [ANZAHL] SEO-Probleme auf ihrer Website gefunden
- Einen kostenlosen Tipp gegeben
- Ein 15-Minuten-Gespräch angeboten

Erstelle 3 Follow-up-E-Mails:

FOLLOW-UP 1 (Tag 3 nach Erstmail):
- Sehr kurz (unter 60 Wörter)
- Bezug auf die erste E-Mail
- Einen NEUEN Insight über ihre SEO hinzufügen
- Kein Druck

FOLLOW-UP 2 (Tag 7 nach Erstmail):
- Kurz (unter 80 Wörter)
- Einen konkreten Mehrwert bieten: z.B. "Ich habe eine kurze Analyse erstellt"
- Social Proof wenn möglich (Ergebnis eines anderen Kunden)
- Nochmal das Gespräch anbieten

FOLLOW-UP 3 (Tag 14 nach Erstmail):
- Sehr kurz (unter 50 Wörter)
- "Breakup-E-Mail" - letzte Nachricht, kein Druck
- Tür offen lassen für die Zukunft
- Menschlich und verständnisvoll

Alle auf Deutsch. Professionell aber nicht steif.
```

## PROMPT 5: Weekly Pipeline Review

Run every Friday to track your outreach and plan next week.

```
Here's my SEO client outreach data for this week:

Emails sent: [NUMBER]
Replies received: [NUMBER]
Positive replies: [NUMBER]
Calls booked: [NUMBER]
Calls completed: [NUMBER]

Breakdown by business type:
- [Type 1]: [sent] sent, [replies] replies
- [Type 2]: [sent] sent, [replies] replies
- [Type 3]: [sent] sent, [replies] replies

Analyze:
1. What's my reply rate? Is it healthy (aim for 5-15% for cold email)?
2. Which business types are responding best?
3. What should I change in my approach next week?
4. Based on the responding types, suggest 10 new prospects to target next week.

Be direct. If something isn't working, tell me why and what to change.
```

## SE Ranking Quick Audit Checklist

When you audit a prospect's website, capture these specific data points
(takes 2-3 minutes per site in SE Ranking):

- [ ] Overall website health score
- [ ] Number of critical errors
- [ ] Number of warnings
- [ ] Page speed score (mobile)
- [ ] Missing meta descriptions (how many pages)
- [ ] Missing title tags
- [ ] Broken links count
- [ ] Mobile-friendliness issues
- [ ] SSL/HTTPS status
- [ ] Google Business Profile: claimed? Optimized? Reviews?

**Pick the 3 most impactful problems** for your outreach email.
Choose problems the business owner can understand without technical knowledge:
- "Your website takes 8 seconds to load on mobile" (they get this)
- "You have 47 pages with duplicate canonical tags" (they don't get this)

## Daily/Weekly Schedule

**Monday (30 min):**
- Find 10 new prospects from Google page 2-3
- Run SE Ranking audits
- Generate outreach emails with Claude
- Send all 10

**Wednesday (10 min):**
- Send Follow-up 1 to prospects from last week Monday
- Check for replies

**Friday (15 min):**
- Send Follow-up 2 where needed
- Run weekly pipeline review prompt
- Update tracking spreadsheet
- Plan next Monday's target business types

**End of Week 2 (10 min):**
- Send Follow-up 3 (breakup emails) to non-responders
- Archive or mark as "revisit in 3 months"
