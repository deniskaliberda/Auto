## PROMPT 2: Generate Ad Variations (Core Prompt - Use Biweekly)

```
Write 5 Meta ad variations for my pro-am dance studio.

Studio details:
- Name: [STUDIO NAME]
- Location: [CITY]
- What we offer: pro-am dance lessons (adults dance with professional instructors)
- Target audience: adults 30-60, likely interested in new hobbies, fitness,
  social activities, or dance specifically
- Current offer/CTA: [e.g., "Free trial lesson", "50% off first month",
  "Book an intro class"]
- Language: [German/English]

Each ad needs:
1. Hook (first line - must stop scrolling in under 2 seconds)
2. Primary text (body copy, under 90 words)
3. Headline (under 40 characters)
4. Description (under 30 characters)
5. Recommended CTA button (Learn More / Sign Up / Book Now / Send Message)
6. Visual direction (what image or video would pair best)

The 5 ads should each take a DIFFERENT psychological angle:

AD 1 - SOCIAL/CONNECTION angle:
Focus on meeting people, date night, making friends, community.
Target emotion: loneliness/desire for connection.

AD 2 - FEAR/COURAGE angle:
Directly address the fear of being a beginner. "Never danced before? Perfect."
Target emotion: anxiety about starting → relief that it's welcome.

AD 3 - TRANSFORMATION angle:
Before/after framing. Where a student started vs. where they are now.
Target emotion: aspiration, "that could be me."

AD 4 - URGENCY/EVENT angle:
Tied to something specific: upcoming showcase, limited spots, new session starting.
Target emotion: FOMO, don't miss out.

AD 5 - IDENTITY/LIFESTYLE angle:
Position dance as part of who they are/want to be. "You've always wanted to..."
Target emotion: self-actualization, fulfilling a dream.

Important:
- These are for cold audiences who may not know our studio exists
- Don't use dance jargon
- The hook MUST work without the image (text-first scrolling)
- Every ad should feel like a real person talking, not a corporation advertising
```

## PROMPT 3: Retargeting Ad Variations

For people who visited your website or engaged with your content but didn't convert.

```
Write 3 Meta retargeting ads for my pro-am dance studio.

These ads target people who:
- Visited our website but didn't book
- Engaged with our Instagram/Facebook but haven't taken action
- Watched our video ads but didn't click through

They already know who we are. Now we need to convert them.

Studio: [NAME], [CITY]
Offer: [YOUR CURRENT OFFER]
Language: [LANGUAGE]

AD 1 - SOCIAL PROOF:
Use testimonials or numbers. "Join 200+ adults who discovered dance at [Studio]."
Make them feel like they're missing out on something others already enjoy.

AD 2 - OBJECTION CRUSHER:
Address the #1 reason they didn't book: [GUESS - probably "I have no rhythm" or
"I'll look stupid" or "I don't have time" or "It's too expensive"]
Disarm the objection directly in the hook.

AD 3 - DIRECT OFFER:
Simple, clear, no-risk offer. Free trial, money-back guarantee, no commitment.
Remove every barrier between them and walking through the door.

For each ad:
1. Hook (first line)
2. Primary text (under 70 words - shorter for retargeting)
3. Headline
4. CTA button
5. Visual direction
```

## PROMPT 4: Video Ad Scripts

Video performs best on Meta. Use these when you can film at the studio.

```
Write 3 short video ad scripts for my pro-am dance studio.
Each video should be 15-30 seconds (Meta's sweet spot).

VIDEO 1: "Day in the Life" (15 seconds)
- Show what actually happens at the studio
- Target: people who don't know what pro-am dance is
- Vibe: warm, fun, welcoming, diverse group of people

VIDEO 2: "Student Story" (30 seconds)
- Interview format with a real student
- They share: why they were nervous, what surprised them, what they love now
- Target: people who relate to the nervousness

VIDEO 3: "First Lesson" (20 seconds)
- Walk someone through exactly what their first lesson looks like
- Demystify the process - remove fear of the unknown
- End with the moment they actually start dancing and smile

For each video, give me:
1. Opening hook (what appears in the first 2 seconds - text overlay)
2. Shot-by-shot breakdown with timing
3. Text overlays for each shot
4. Voiceover script (or whether to use music only)
5. End card: what text + CTA
6. Caption for the post

Keep the energy real and authentic. The best-performing studio ads
look like they were filmed on an iPhone during a real lesson.
```

## A/B Testing Framework

Follow this system to continuously improve your ads:

### The Rules:
1. Always have a "champion" (your best performing ad) running
2. Test 1-2 "challengers" against the champion at a time
3. Run each test for minimum 7 days OR until you have 100+ clicks per ad
4. The metric that matters most: **cost per lead** (not CTR, not impressions)
5. Kill the loser. The winner becomes the new champion.
6. Write a new challenger and repeat.

### What to Test (in this order):
1. **Hook/first line** (biggest impact - test this first)
2. **Angle** (social vs. fear vs. transformation etc.)
3. **Visual** (video vs. image, people vs. text-based)
4. **CTA** (Book Now vs. Learn More vs. Send Message)
5. **Audience** (age segments, interest targeting)
6. **Placement** (let Meta optimize first, then test manual placement)

### Testing Log (keep in a spreadsheet):

| Week | Champion | Challenger | Days Run | Champion CPL | Challenger CPL | Winner | Learning |
|------|----------|------------|----------|--------------|----------------|--------|----------|

### Key Benchmarks for Dance Studio Ads:
- CTR above 1% = decent
- CTR above 2% = strong
- CPL under €15 = good for local service businesses (adjust for your market)
- If CPL rises 30%+ over 2 weeks on the same ad = creative fatigue, time for new creative

## Quick Ad Copy Formulas

When you need a fast hook, use these fill-in-the-blank formulas with Claude:

**PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve):**
"Tired of [problem]? [Make it worse]. [Your studio] is the answer."

**Before-After-Bridge:**
"Before: [relatable situation]. After: [aspirational result]. The bridge? [Your studio]."

**Social Proof Lead:**
"[X] adults in [City] already discovered [result]. Here's what they know that you don't."

**Direct Question:**
"When's the last time you [aspirational activity]? It's been too long."

**Myth Buster:**
"You don't need [common objection: rhythm/partner/experience] to [desired outcome]."

**Curiosity Gap:**
"The #1 thing new dance students say after their first lesson: [surprising quote]."
